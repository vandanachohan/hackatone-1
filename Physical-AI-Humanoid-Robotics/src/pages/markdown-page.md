# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the official textbook page for the **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Course**.

This course is part of **Hackathon I**, where the goal is to create a complete and modern textbook to teach the next generation of engineers, developers, and innovators.

---

## 📘 About This Textbook
The future of work will be a collaboration between **humans, AI agents, and humanoid robots**. Instead of replacing jobs, this shift will *reshape* what work looks like.

This textbook helps students build the skills needed to thrive in this AI-powered future.

---

## 📚 What You Will Learn
- 🤖 Foundations of Physical AI
- 🦾 Humanoid robot hardware & sensors
- 🧠 AI agents controlling physical systems
- ⚙️ Control theory & locomotion
- 🔌 Embedded systems & ROS
- 🏗️ Simulation tools for robotics
- 🚀 Future-ready skills for the AI-robot workforce
