# Foundations of Physical AI

Physical AI represents a fundamental shift from traditional artificial intelligence systems that operate purely in digital spaces to AI systems that are embodied and interact with the physical world. This chapter establishes the foundational concepts that underpin all physical AI and humanoid robotics applications.

## What is Embodied AI?

Embodied AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that exist within a physical form and interact with the real world. Unlike traditional AI systems that process data in isolation, embodied AI:

- Perceives its environment through sensors
- Acts upon the environment through actuators
- Learns from physical interactions
- Adapts to real-world constraints and dynamics

## Core Components of Physical AI Systems

### Perception Systems
Physical AI systems rely on various sensors to understand their environment:
- Visual sensors (cameras, LIDAR, depth sensors)
- Tactile sensors for touch and force feedback
- Auditory sensors for sound recognition
- Proprioceptive sensors to understand their own body state

### Cognition and Planning
Physical AI must process sensory information and plan actions considering:
- Environmental constraints
- Physical laws (dynamics, kinematics)
- Safety requirements
- Task objectives

### Actuation Systems
Actuators enable physical AI to interact with the world through:
- Motor control systems
- Manipulation mechanisms
- Locomotion systems
- Human-robot interfaces

## The Embodiment Hypothesis

The embodiment hypothesis suggests that intelligence emerges from the interaction between an agent and its environment. This means that:

- Physical interaction shapes cognitive development
- Environmental affordances influence behavior
- Sensorimotor experiences are essential for true understanding
- Physical constraints drive efficient solutions

## Learning Objectives

2. Research the concept of "affordances" in robotics. How do physical AI systems recognize and utilize affordances in their environment?
3. Design a simple physical AI system for a specific task. What sensors, actuators, and cognitive components would it need?
